Oh look around and you shall see,
The numbers waiting to be found.
But where they are or what they be
These clues will help you to unwound.

Now, first of all your eyes must find,
A stone for castles children build.
This cube will often bring to mind
The play of fingers yet unskilled.

The second number hides within
A potted thing that dreams of sun.
It thinks of what it might have been,
If it in freedom had been sprung.

Prepare to now a third unsheath,
It is within a vision furled.
A mouth that speaks yet does not breathe.
A window into many a world.

And now, at last, your eyes must turn
To colors trapped in silent splendor.
It’s scent is lost and shan’t return
Yet still a gift it shall surrender.

The fifth letter is quite the test
Of expertise with navigation.
A seeker small must make a quest
To reach a prize of sweet temptation.

The final digit you must trace
Hides in a world that’s bound in ink
Where paper dreams are locked in place
And spines conceal what others think.

And now, at last!, the numbers clear,
The final truth must be bestowed.
The key is yours, the end is near,
The lock awaits – now test your code!
